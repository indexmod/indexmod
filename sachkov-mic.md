**Mic Sachkov** (Mikhail Sachkov) is a Russian artist, filmmaker, and media artist associated with video art, fashion film, media strategy, digital production, and immersive audiovisual projects. His work connects commercial moving-image production with contemporary media art, including audiovisual performance, projection, digital editions, and experimental film. **[1]** **[2]**

{{page:image}}

*Image: Facebook-hosted image. Source URL provided by editor.*

## Career

Sachkov worked with **MTV Russia**, producing animated promotional material and later working as an art director. This early work placed him within the development of post-Soviet Russian television, youth media, and branded visual culture. **[1]**

He became known as one of the Russian practitioners connected with **fashion film** and contemporary moving-image culture. SHOWstudio lists him as a Moscow-based contributor, reflecting his relationship to fashion-image platforms and experimental film contexts. **[3]**

## 4S4R And Phosphor

In 2010 Sachkov co-founded **4S4R**, also known as **Phosphor**, a Moscow-based collective of video artists. The collective worked with audiovisual performance, abstract graphics, projection, and multimedia installation. Its projects explored a minimal visual language and the relationship between image, sound, architecture, and temporary exhibition formats. **[1]**

Projects associated with 4S4R include pop-up projection events, site-specific audiovisual works, and collaborations with cultural institutions. The original article listed **Bring Your Projector**, **Transmutation / Cyclotron II**, **Digital Dreams about Russia**, and audiovisual work for *L'Illusion du Marriage* as key projects connected with the collective.

## Artistic Practice

Sachkov's practice moves between several fields:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Video art** | Experimental moving-image works, visual narratives, and audiovisual compositions |
| **Digital art** | Limited digital editions, screen-based works, and platform-based art objects |
| **Installation** | Immersive environments using projection, image, sound, and space |
| **Fashion film** | Moving-image work connected with fashion, style, performance, and brand culture |
| **Media strategy** | Commercial and cultural projects shaped by digital production and visual communication |

The recurring themes in his work include urban perception, digital surfaces, nightlife, technology, architectural space, and the shifting boundary between commercial image-making and independent media art. **[1]** **[4]**

## Independent Work

Since the late 2010s, Sachkov has worked as an independent video artist and media producer. His public platforms include digital art marketplaces and video archives. Artsy and Sedition list works and artist pages under the name Mic Sachkov, placing his production within contemporary digital-art distribution as well as broader online art-market systems. **[2]** **[4]**

His YouTube archive includes urban video material, travel records, nightlife, and documentary-style fragments from cities including Tokyo and Seoul. These materials extend his interest in the moving image as a way to observe contemporary environments. **[5]**
