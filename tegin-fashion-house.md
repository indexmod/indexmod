**Tegin Fashion House** is a Russian fashion house founded by designer **Svetlana Tegin** in 2001. The brand is known for women's clothing, cashmere collections, atelier work, accessories, and creative projects connected with cinema, theatre, performance, and art. **[1]**

## History

Tegin Fashion House was founded in Moscow in 2001. The brand developed around Svetlana Tegin's interest in clothing, costume, cultural references, and performance-based presentation. Not Just A Label describes Tegin as one of the more widely recognised Russian fashion designers of her generation and notes that the fashion house opened in 2001. **[2]**

The house built its identity through ready-to-wear collections, cashmere garments, childrenswear, and custom pieces. Its public image is closely connected with Svetlana Tegin's reputation for cashmere and with runway presentations that often use theatrical or cinematic atmosphere rather than conventional commercial staging. **[1]** **[2]**

## Lines And Activities

The official Tegin company page presents the fashion house as working in five main directions: **Tegin Creative**, **Tegin Cashmere**, **Tegin Collection**, **Tegin Atelier**, and **Tegin's Angel**. **[1]**

**Tegin Creative** covers the designer's activity in cinema, theatre projects, collaborations, and art performances. The brand states that Svetlana Tegin has designed costumes for projects including the series *Vertinsky* and the play *Einstein and Margarita*. **[1]**

**Tegin Cashmere** is one of the central parts of the brand. The company describes its cashmere collections as made from Mongolian cashmere, with production connected to a factory in Mongolia and authorial oversight by Svetlana Tegin. **[1]**

**Tegin Collection** refers to seasonal collections released by the brand. The official page lists collection titles such as *Irrealism*, *Dream*, *Ice Nymphs*, *Homunculus G*, *Dolls*, *Mongol*, *Retrofuture*, *Clones*, *Birds*, and *Japanese Tales Whispered in Icelandic*. **[1]**

**Tegin Atelier** provides custom garments made to individual measurements, while **Tegin's Angel** is described as a line of semi-wool accessories with hand finishing and a focus on shine. **[1]**

## Retail And Distribution

Tegin sells through its online store and through boutiques in Russia. The official company page lists Moscow stockists including TSUM, LEFORM, Rehabshop, Tsvetnoy, and PARAD, and Saint Petersburg stockists including DLT and Krasny Most. **[1]**

The brand has also presented collections at international fashion exhibitions and fashion weeks. The official page lists platforms including WHITE Milano, Tranoi in Paris, Coterie in New York, CPD in Dusseldorf, CPM in Moscow, Ukrainian Fashion Week in Kyiv, Moscow Fashion Week, and Mercedes-Benz Fashion Week Russia. **[1]**

## Style

Tegin's design language is often described through restraint, romance, natural materials, and cultural reference. Not Just A Label links Svetlana Tegin's style to intellectual and philosophical content, world cultural heritage, and a balance between sensuality and restraint. **[2]**

The house's public identity also depends on the combination of fashion and performance. Rather than limiting the brand to seasonal clothing, Tegin uses costume, moving image, theatre, and art collaboration as part of the wider language of the fashion house.
