***Updated 2026-08-15***

**Identity of Indexmod** is the visual identity system of Indexmod, an encyclopedia of art and fashion established in Moscow in 2006. The identity developed around the recurring motif of an **eye**, first appearing in an earlier 2011 image by Andrei Abramov, also known as Odinmay, and later reworked by Andrei Deinichenko for Indexmod's sign system in 2015.

{{page:image}}

*Image: Indexmod identity asset. Source: /logo.svg*

## Development

The Indexmod identity uses a sequence of signs that combine the eye motif with initials, circles, orbital forms, waves, and sphere-like geometry. These images link the project to ideas of observation, indexing, encyclopedic attention, and visual navigation.

The 2011 version, described as an **Eye on "IM"**, introduced the eye as a central mark. In 2015, the motif was transformed into a gold eye sign by Andrei Deinichenko. Later versions expanded the identity through waves, orbit-like structures, and a sphere with the letter **I**.

## Interpretation

The identity is built around a compact visual language rather than a single fixed logo. The repeated eye suggests seeing, selection, and attention, while the later orbit and sphere forms connect the mark to systems, archives, networks, and encyclopedic structure.

Because Indexmod works across art, fashion, cultural history, and media, the identity functions as both a sign and a diagram: it marks the project while also suggesting the movement of references around a central point of observation.
