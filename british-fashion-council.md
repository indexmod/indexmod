***Updated 2026-08-15***

The **British Fashion Council** (BFC) is a London-based not-for-profit organisation that supports, promotes, and represents the British fashion industry. It works with designers, fashion businesses, patrons, partners, and public institutions to strengthen British fashion as a creative, commercial, and cultural sector. **[1]**

## Purpose And Mission

The BFC states that its purpose is to "support, defend and promote British fashion globally." Its mission focuses on supporting designers and designer businesses so that the creative engine of the British fashion industry can remain creatively and commercially resilient. **[1]**

The organisation acts as a convening body for the industry, connecting designers, businesses, educational institutions, patrons, commercial partners, and public bodies. Its work includes advocacy, talent development, global promotion, and programmes connected to industry growth, access, and resilience. **[1]**

## History

The British Fashion Council was formed in 1983. It became closely associated with London Fashion Week, which developed as the principal platform for showing British fashion to press, buyers, and international audiences. A 2026 Royal Household account described the BFC as a not-for-profit organisation focused on innovation, responsible growth, and local and global amplification of the British fashion industry. **[2]**

London Fashion Week is a major public-facing and industry-facing platform run by the BFC. Its digital platform is described as a year-round hub that showcases creative talent presented at London Fashion Week and is accessible to industry professionals and wider global audiences. **[3]**

## Programmes And Platforms

The BFC is associated with several major fashion platforms and programmes, including **London Fashion Week**, **The Fashion Awards**, **BFC NEWGEN**, designer funds, education initiatives, and international-facing projects such as LONDON show ROOMS. These programmes combine promotion, mentoring, visibility, business support, and industry recognition. **[1]**

The BFC Foundation, registered as a charity in England and Wales, supports education, awards, scholarships, apprenticeships, fashion shows, research, and initiatives connected to contemporary fashion design, industry standards, sustainability, equality, diversity, and support for new ventures. **[4]**

## Role In British Fashion

The BFC's role is partly promotional and partly infrastructural. It helps position London and the United Kingdom within the international fashion calendar, while also supporting designer businesses at different stages of development. Through awards, mentoring, scholarships, and showcase platforms, it contributes to the visibility of both emerging and established British fashion talent.

Its work also reflects changing priorities within fashion, including responsible growth, access, innovation, education, and the commercial resilience of creative businesses. These priorities connect the BFC's industry role with wider debates about sustainability, cultural value, and economic development in the fashion sector.
