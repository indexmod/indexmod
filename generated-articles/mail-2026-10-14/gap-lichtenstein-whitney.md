***Updated 2026-10-14***

Gap–Lichtenstein–Whitney is an eighteen-piece clothing and accessories collection created by Gap in collaboration with the Whitney Museum of American Art and the Estate of Roy Lichtenstein. Released on 7 October 2026, it accompanied the New York museum's exhibition *Roy Lichtenstein: Like New*. [1]

{{page:image}}

{{page:credit}}

## Design and distribution

The collection places reproductions and adaptations of Lichtenstein's images on everyday garments. Works represented include *Crying Girl* (1963), *Standing Explosion (Black)* (1965) and *Cathedral #2* (1969). Products include hoodies, shirts, graphic T-shirts and a varsity jacket, alongside accessories such as a cap, scarf and knitted throw. Distribution began through selected Gap shops, Gap's website and the Whitney Museum Shop. [1]

Gap's product documentation identifies a heavyweight hoodie with *Standing Explosion*-inspired detailing on its back. The garment demonstrates the project's use of a familiar apparel format as a surface for the artist's imagery. The brand identifies both the estate and the Whitney as its collaborators. [2]

## Relationship to Lichtenstein's work

Lichtenstein's practice drew on commercial imagery while using painstaking manual processes to reproduce the appearance of printed dots, flat colours and heavy outlines. The Whitney's account of his work stresses the difference between its industrial appearance and the labour involved in producing it. [3]

The collection coincides with an exhibition devoted to the ways images are borrowed, enlarged and recirculated. This provides a specific art-historical context for the collaboration: imagery associated with Lichtenstein's reconsideration of mass culture returns to circulation through manufactured clothing. The collection is a licensed collaboration with the artist's estate, rather than a new body of work by Lichtenstein. [1][2][4]
