***Updated 2026-10-14***

ADL Fashion Week is a fashion festival in Adelaide, South Australia, presented by the Adelaide Economic Development Agency, a subsidiary of the City of Adelaide. It combines runway presentations with independently produced events involving design, retail and the city's wider creative industries. Its fifth edition was scheduled for 7–11 October 2026, with support from the South Australian Fashion Industry Association. [1]

{{page:image}}

{{page:credit}}

## Format

The 2026 programme comprised five headline runways and more than thirty events across Adelaide and North Adelaide. Alongside designer showcases, the festival included workshops, conversations, shopping, hospitality and sustainability-related activities. This structure places fashion in a city-wide setting, linking presentations of clothing to venues and businesses beyond the runway. [1]

Adelaide Town Hall hosted headline presentations, while events elsewhere included *Threaded: Adelaide* at the Piccadilly, the SAFIA Industry Breakfast at Patina Place and a jewellery-design workshop at Shirley Wu Jewellery. The published programme combined free public activities with ticketed events. [2]

## Craft and fashion culture

The 2026 programme included the Australian premiere of *Handmade Future*, a documentary about traditional craft and the contemporary conditions of artisan production. Its screening at the Mercury Cinema on 10 October was followed by a discussion involving Olivia, Mel Flintoft of Silver Fleece and designer Rachel Van Brussel. A separate walking tour on 9 October explored businesses associated with slow fashion. [2][3]

The Wolf Gang's tenth-anniversary presentation was another component of the programme. The label announced its first solo runway for 10 October, styled and curated by founder Vanessa Pearce. Pearce also appeared in the festival's *The Threads Between Us* campaign, which used five portraits to describe different relationships with Adelaide's creative community. [4]
