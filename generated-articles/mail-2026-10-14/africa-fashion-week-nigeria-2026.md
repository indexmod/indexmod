***Updated 2026-10-14***

Africa Fashion Week Nigeria 2026 is the announced 2026 edition of the Lagos fashion platform supporting emerging African designers through runway shows, exhibitions and professional networking. Organisers scheduled the event for 12–13 December 2026; as of 14 October it remained forthcoming. [1][2]

{{page:image}}

{{page:credit}}

## Background

Ronke Ademiluyi founded the Nigerian event in 2014 as a sister platform to Africa Fashion Week London. Contemporary reporting places its inaugural edition at Eko Hotel and Suites on 18 May 2014, with emerging designers joined by established labels. This history distinguishes the Nigerian event from the similarly named London programme. [3][4]

## Programme and commercial focus

The organisers' 2026 registration material invites designers, exhibitors and media participants. Public announcements frame the Lagos edition around connecting African creative businesses with local audiences and visitors from the diaspora during the December season. These are stated objectives of the event, rather than evidence of completed sales or attendance. [1][5]

An organiser newsletter dated 7 October announced a Buyers Programme for retailers, stockists and distributors. It described introductions to designers, business meetings and follow-up support, covering womenswear, menswear, accessories and contemporary interpretations of African craft. The same announcement identified the John Randle Museum for Yoruba Culture and History in Onikan, Lagos Island, as the planned venue. These programme details are attributed to the organiser's announcement and remain subject to change. [6]

The proposed buying component gives the edition a business-to-business purpose alongside its public-facing runway and exhibition activities. No final participant list, visitor total or commercial results are established by the cited announcements. [1][5][6]
