***Updated 2026-10-14***

*Handmade Future* is a 2026 documentary directed by Kirsten Dickerson and produced by ARTIST & NOMAD. Made as a United Kingdom–United States production, it follows artisans in six countries and considers traditional craft, cultural inheritance and the economic conditions of making. Its subject connects textile and design practices with questions of trade and the preservation of skills. [1][2]

{{page:image}}

{{page:credit}}

## Production and subjects

Kirsten and Brandon Dickerson produced the film, with Brandon also serving as director of photography. The filmmakers' published credits name Jonny Elwyn as editor and Peter Schottleutner as composer. The listed running time is approximately seventy-three minutes. [1][2]

Filming took place in India, Kenya, Mexico, Morocco, the United Kingdom and the United States. Subjects include rug weaving in Morocco, block printing in India, broom making in Kentucky and recycled-glass work in Kenya. The film's stated focus is the relationship between inherited knowledge, intermediaries and responsible trade, rather than craft solely as a finished luxury product. [2]

## Screenings and fashion context

The filmmakers' festival listing records a world premiere at the Justice Film Festival in New York on 26 February 2026. A London screening on 12 September was presented with a discussion involving Dickerson and textile designer Celia Birchall, alongside objects made by artisans featured in the film. [2][3]

ADL Fashion Week programmed the Australian premiere at Adelaide's Mercury Cinema for 10 October 2026. Its associated discussion addressed craftsmanship, ethics and fashion's future. The screening placed a film about international artisan production within a festival programme also concerned with local makers and slow fashion. [4][5]
